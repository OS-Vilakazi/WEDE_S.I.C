Why S.I.C

Cinema was never just about the film.

It's the dark room, the big sound, the strangers who cheer with you. We're bringing that back — for sport.

01 / Sight & sound

Stadium-grade screen and sound

Every kick, lap and goal on a screen that dwarfs your TV, with sound designed for the room, not a soundbar.

02 / Atmosphere

A crowd, not a couch

Watch alongside other fans instead of alone at home — the reactions are half the reason to go.

03 / No clash with movies

Off-peak screening slots

We use cinema halls during quieter hours, so screenings never compete with the evening's film schedule.
