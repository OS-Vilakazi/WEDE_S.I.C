Enquiries

Sponsor a screening or book a group

Whether you're a brand looking to reach match-day crowds, or a supporters' club after your own screen — tell us what you need.

What we can help with

  • Sponsorship — brand visibility at screenings, on-screen mentions, and activation space in the cinema foyer.
  • Group bookings — block seating for supporters' clubs, offices or friend groups (10+ seats).
  • Private screen hire — a full screen for your organisation on a specific fixture.
  • General questions — anything about upcoming fixtures, pricing or locations.

We usually respond within 2 working days. For anything urgent, use the contact details on the Contact page.
