Why We Built S.I.C

South African cinema attendance has been falling for years as streaming pulls audiences home. We think the fix isn't a new film — it's a new reason to leave the house.

Our Story

S.I.C (Sport in Cinema) partners with Ster-Kinekor, South Africa's largest cinema exhibitor, to use quiet daytime and weekend slots productively: screening live Formula 1 races and football matches on full cinema screens, with proper sound and a room full of fellow fans.

Mission

To turn the cinema back into a lively social destination by giving sports fans a big-screen, big-atmosphere alternative to watching alone at home.

Vision

To become the go-to South African venue for watching major sporting events outside of stadiums and sports bars.

Who It's For

  • Motorsport and football fans aged 18–40 who want a social viewing experience.
  • Friend groups and supporters' clubs looking for a venue for match days and race weekends.
  • Regular Ster-Kinekor customers given a new reason to come back to the cinema.
