About Us

Why we built S.I.C

South African cinema attendance has been falling for years as streaming pulls audiences home. We think the fix isn't a new film — it's a new reason to leave the house.

Our story

S.I.C (Sport in Cinema) started with a simple observation: cinemas sit half-empty during the day and on weekend mornings, while sports fans crowd into pubs, sports bars and living rooms to watch the same fixtures on much smaller screens.

We partner with Ster-Kinekor — South Africa's largest cinema exhibitor — to use that unused time productively: screening live Formula 1 races and football matches on full cinema screens, with proper sound and a room full of fellow fans.

The goal was never to make sport more popular. It was already popular. The goal is to make the cinema itself feel alive again — noisy, social, and worth leaving the house for.

Mission & vision

Mission

To turn the cinema back into a lively social destination by giving sports fans a big-screen, big-atmosphere alternative to watching alone at home.

Vision

To become the go-to South African venue for watching major sporting events outside of stadiums and sports bars.

How a screening works

  • Book your seat online for a listed F1 or football fixture.
  • Arrive 20 minutes early — doors open with pre-match atmosphere and concessions.
  • Watch the full broadcast, live, on a full cinema screen with cinema sound.
  • Reactions included: cheering, groaning and everything in between is part of the ticket.
